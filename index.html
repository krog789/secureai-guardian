Capabilities

Built to understand, not just filter

Keyword filters catch what they've seen before. SecureAI Guardian is built around actual comprehension, so novel attacks and disguised requests don't slip through.

Semantic AI understanding

Prompts are read for real meaning and intent using a connected AI model — local via Ollama or cloud-hosted — not just scanned for trigger words.

9-layer detection engine

Every prompt passes through tokenization, behavioral analysis, semantic relationships, and pattern matching before a verdict is reached.

Governance you can defend

Every decision comes with the specific rule that triggered it, so you can explain any Allow, Warn, Restrict, or Block to an auditor.

Complete audit trail

Every analysis is logged with timestamp, risk score, and reasoning, private to each signed-in account and ready to export.

Local or cloud AI

Run entirely on your own hardware with Ollama for full data control, or use the built-in cloud backend so it works for every visitor instantly.

Real accounts, private data

Every user signs in with their own account. History, settings, and results never mix between people using the same deployment.

Workflow

From prompt to verdict, in four steps

The same pipeline runs on every single request, whether it's a test in the dashboard or live traffic from your application.

01

Prompt submitted

A prompt is entered manually or arrives through your application, exactly as a user or system wrote it.

02

AI reads the meaning

A connected model interprets intent — recognizing disguised requests, roleplay framing, and social engineering, not just flagged words.

03

9 layers score it

Tokenization, behavioral indicators, semantic relationships, and pattern matching each contribute to a single risk score and category.

04

Decision & audit log

Allow, Warn, Restrict, or Block is returned instantly, with the governance rule applied and a permanent record in your audit log.

FAQ

Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out on the contact form below.

Does the AI actually understand meaning, or just match keywords?
A connected AI model reads the full prompt for intent — including disguised or roleplay-framed requests — while the 9-layer engine adds keyword, pattern, and behavioral checks as a second layer of coverage.
Can I run this without sending data to the cloud?
Yes. Switch the AI mode to Local Ollama in Settings and every analysis runs entirely on your own hardware, with nothing leaving your network.
Is my data shared with other users?
No. Every account has its own private history and settings, stored separately in the database and never visible to other accounts.
What happens if the AI model is unreachable?
Analysis automatically falls back to the local rule-based engine, so you always get a result — the dashboard clearly labels which engine produced it.
